Original SAT-style example

A club has $24 saved and adds $8 each week. After how many weeks will it have $80?

  1. A5 weeks
  2. B6 weeks
  3. C7 weeks Correct
  4. D10 weeks
One step clearer. Solve 24 + 8w = 80. Subtract 24, then divide by 8: w = 7.

A few things to know

Good questions.

Who can use Road to 1600?

It is intended for Collegium Charter School SAT prep club members ages 13 and up. Google accounts ending in @ccs.us are automatically approved. All other Google emails receive provisional access pending Leon’s membership review; access may later be denied. Access is not limited to the school email domain.

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no payments, subscriptions, ads, sponsors, or sales. This is a private, non-profit, student-run club resource.

What can I use today?

The question bank, practice exams, mistake review, notes, and embedded Desmos calculator exist today. Guided lessons, slides, and live responses are coming soon and are not currently available.

Is this an official SAT or school platform?

No. Road to 1600 is independent of the College Board and is not an official Collegium Charter School service. Practice scores are estimates and do not predict or guarantee your official score.

What happens to my practice data?

The app saves account details and study data such as answers, progress, notes, settings, and exam sessions. Read the Privacy Policy for storage, providers, and retention limits that still need to be finalized.

Need help with access or your account?

Email Leon at chakrabortyleon@gmail.com. If your account is under review, include the Google email you used to sign in. Never send your password.
