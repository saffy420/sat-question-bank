Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Road to 1600 (roadto1600.org) is a private, non-profit, student-run SAT prep club resource operated by Leon, a Collegium Charter School student. It is not an official school service. Contact: chakrabortyleon@gmail.com.

A good-faith description, not a compliance certification. This policy explains the app’s data practices and known gaps. Retention outside exam sessions, provider logs and backups, deletion procedures and timing, analytics verification, and change-announcement procedures remain to be finalized. We do not claim FERPA or COPPA certification or compliance based on this policy.

1. Who this is for

This resource is intended for Collegium Charter School SAT prep club members ages 13 and up. Do not use it if you are under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information, contact Leon so the account and information can be reviewed for removal.

Sign-in is Google-only. @ccs.us accounts are automatically approved. All other Google emails receive provisional access pending Leon’s review of membership; access can subsequently be denied. This is not a school-domain-only restriction.

2. Information stored

The app uses Cloudflare D1 for account and study records. Depending on how you use the app, these include:

  • Account: user ID, email address, name, and account creation timestamp.
  • Membership: user ID, email address, approved, pending, or denied status, a membership creation timestamp (created_at), and an optional review timestamp (reviewed_at). The review timestamp may be empty before a review is recorded.
  • Question progress: question IDs, attempt and correct-answer counts, progress markers, timestamps, time spent, and stars.
  • Attempt history: question IDs, answer text or answer letter, correctness, time spent, timestamps, and answer-change data.
  • Notes: text you save for questions.
  • Settings: theme, retry preferences, random question count, unused AI instruction text, the noteOnWrong preference, and other JSON settings supplied by the app. The settings record can hold arbitrary JSON, so do not put sensitive information in it.
  • Exam sessions: answers, flagged questions, timing, current position, scores, and review state.

Authentication providers also process sign-in information. Hosting and delivery providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, request details, browser information, and logs. Their exact logging and backup retention for this deployment has not yet been confirmed.

3. How information is used

Account information supports sign-in, account identification, and membership review. Study records allow the app to save your practice, show progress and attempt history, restore exam sessions, and remember notes and preferences. Leon may review membership information and handle access, support, and deletion requests.

There are no ads, payments, sponsors, or sales. We do not sell personal information. No analytics are intended; however, verification that live Cloudflare analytics are disabled is still pending. We cannot yet promise that no platform analytics are collected.

4. Cookies and browser storage

After Google sign-in through Supabase, the app sends the access token to its session endpoint to verify identity and membership. Successful verification sets the __Host-sat_session authentication cookie with HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax, and Path=/. Its lifetime is limited to the remaining access-token lifetime, capped at one hour each time it is set. HttpOnly prevents page JavaScript from reading that cookie; Secure limits transmission to HTTPS; SameSite=Lax limits some cross-site use. These controls do not make an account or device immune to compromise.

The existing Supabase session is persisted in localStorage. App preferences may also be stored there: satq_settings, filters, browse mode, focus, the welcomed flag, and legacy theme values. Local storage persists until cleared by the app or browser. This app does not use sessionStorage.

The public informational pages do not add analytics or preference storage. Google sign-in and the study app use the authentication and service providers described here. On a shared device, sign out and consider clearing site data; clearing browser data does not delete server-side records.

5. Service providers and sharing

  • Google: provides Google account sign-in and processes authentication-related information.
  • Supabase: provides authentication and session handling. Its browser SDK is delivered through jsDelivr.
  • Cloudflare: hosts the site and Worker API and stores account and practice data in D1. It may process operational, security, and request information.
  • jsDelivr, KaTeX, and Roboto delivery: the existing study app loads third-party resources, including the Supabase SDK, KaTeX for math rendering, and Roboto font resources. Those network requests expose technical request information to the relevant delivery providers. The public pages use system fonts and no external images.
  • Desmos: the embedded calculator loads when you open it. Requests then go to Desmos, which may process technical information under its own policies.

These services have their own privacy practices; this policy does not set their retention periods or guarantee their handling. Information may also be disclosed when legally required or needed to investigate misuse and protect accounts.

“Copy for AI” is clipboard export only. It does not send questions or your work to an AI model through this app. If you paste exported content into another service, that service’s terms and privacy practices apply. The unused AI instruction setting is still stored when included in your settings.

6. Retention and deletion

Exam sessions: sessions older than 30 days since their last write are lazily purged when your sessions are next read. This is not a scheduled deletion exactly on day 30; an unread session may remain longer.

Other records: a fixed retention schedule for accounts, membership reviews, progress, attempts, notes, and settings has not yet been finalized. Provider logging and backup retention also remain unconfirmed. Do not assume these records are automatically deleted after 30 days.

Deletion requests: email chakrabortyleon@gmail.com using your account email where possible. Do not send passwords or authentication tokens. Leon will need to verify account ownership and review the request. The detailed deletion process, completion timing, and treatment of provider backups are still to be finalized; no fixed turnaround is promised.

7. Your choices and security

You may stop using the resource, clear local site data, or contact Leon to request access to, correction of, or deletion of account information. Avoid putting personal, confidential, or sensitive information in notes, answers, or settings. Only share what is needed for practice or support.

Authentication and account-scoped storage are used to protect study records, but no service is perfectly secure. Saved work may be lost or unavailable. Signing out does not necessarily invalidate every previously issued access token immediately. Report suspected account misuse to Leon.

8. Policy changes and contact

Updates will be reflected in this page’s “Last updated” date. A separate process for announcing material changes has not yet been finalized; check this page periodically. Questions, privacy concerns, and account requests should go to chakrabortyleon@gmail.com.
